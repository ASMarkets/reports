01The business in thirty seconds

Data as of 24 Feb 2026 · FY2025 10-K · segment revenue shares

What it does

Moves freight across the United States. In its largest business, a customer's load goes into one of the company's own 53-foot containers, a railroad carries it for the long haul, and the company's own trucks handle the pick-up and the delivery.

How it makes money

A price per load in intermodal. In dedicated trucking, contracts of three to ten years (about five on average), priced cost-plus: fixed costs are recovered whether or not the equipment is fully used. Brokerage earns the margin between what shippers pay and what carriers charge.

Products that matter

Intermodal (JBI) is 50% of 2025 segment revenue; Dedicated (DCS) 28%; Integrated Capacity Solutions, the brokerage (ICS), 9%; Final Mile (FMS), 7%; Truckload (JBT), 6%.

Where it operates

The United States. The company states that international operations are "not material" to its financial position, results or cash flows.

05The moat — assets that are physical and hard to copy

Data as of 15 Jul 2026 (Q2 2026) and 24 Feb 2026 (FY2025 10-K)

  • Own containers and chassis. About 124,000 containers (124,199 "trailing equipment" at 30 Jun 2026; 124,838 containers at 31 Dec 2025) and 104,474 chassis at year-end 2025.
  • Own drayage tractors. 6,232 intermodal tractors at 30 Jun 2026 to pick up and deliver at each end of the rail leg.
  • A rail partnership since 1989. The company has agreements with several Class I railroads, but the majority of its business travels on BNSF and Norfolk Southern.
  • Contracted base in Dedicated. Contracts run three to ten years; customer retention was about 96% in Q2 2026.
  • Operating leverage showing. In Q2 2026, intermodal loads grew 10% and intermodal operating income grew 58%, to $150.9 million.

09Business risks

Status as of 16 Sep 2026 (management statement), 19 Aug 2026 (regulatory calendar) and 15 Jul 2026 (results)

Costs

Costs arrive before price

At a Morgan Stanley investor conference in mid-September 2026, management said third-quarter earnings per share would be 5% to 10% below the second quarter ($1.91), citing about $25 million of additional driver recruiting, advertising, training and onboarding costs, and at least $10 million from higher diesel prices. This statement was made at a conference; it is reported by the press and was not filed with the SEC.

Rail dependence

Most volume rides on two railroads, one of which may change owner

The majority of the business travels on BNSF and Norfolk Southern. Union Pacific has proposed to acquire Norfolk Southern. The Surface Transportation Board has set final briefs for 28 May 2027 and a decision is expected in the second half of 2027. The FY2025 10-K does not mention the proposed merger.

Mix

Growth that earns less

Brokerage (ICS) revenue grew 49% in Q2 2026, but its gross profit margin fell to 12.5% from 15.5%. Truckload swung to an operating loss of $1.3 million (Q2 2025: profit of $3.4 million) and Final Mile operating income fell 30%.
