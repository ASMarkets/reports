01The business in thirty seconds

Data as of 31 Dec 2025 · FY2025 10-K and recast segments (8-K, 25 Mar 2026) · segment definitions from the Q2 2026 10-Q

What it does

Designs and manufactures generators, energy storage systems, energy management devices and other power products and services for residential, commercial, data center, telecom, rental and industrial markets. It sells through dealers, distributors, retailers, e-commerce partners, wholesalers and rental companies, and directly to some end users.

How it makes money

Product sales to distribution partners, who sell on to the end customer. Residential revenue also includes aftermarket service parts, amortization of extended-warranty revenue, grid services and remote-monitoring subscriptions. Residential products are sold predominantly through independent residential dealers, national and regional retailers, e-commerce merchants, electrical/HVAC/solar wholesalers, solar installers and outdoor power equipment dealers.

Products that matter

Residential: automatic home standby generators (7.5 kW to 150 kW), portable generators, residential energy storage, energy management devices and outdoor power equipment. Commercial & Industrial (C&I): stationary generators up to 3,250 kW, mobile generators, light towers, C&I battery storage, mobile heaters and pumps.

Where it operates

In 2025, 82.5% of net sales ($3,471m) came from the company's former Domestic segment and 17.5% ($738m) from its International segment (calculated; the company reorganized its segments on 31 March 2026). No single customer provided more than 4% of 2025 net sales.

04What is driving the growth — large generators for data centers

Data as of 29 Jul 2026 (release) · 16 Sep 2026 (8-K) · 30 Jun 2026 (10-Q)

  • Management says backlog for products serving the data center market was about $1.6 billion as of 29 July 2026, and that it does not include any committed volumes from a second hyperscale customer. The company also said product-specific terms with one hyperscale customer "committed nearly $700 million of volume for 2027".
  • Supply agreement, 16 Sep 2026. The company entered a long-term supply agreement with a major data center customer, with initial deliveries expected to total $2.4 billion in 2027 and 2028. The filing does not say whether this is the second hyperscale customer referred to in the July release.
  • Warrant. The company issued that customer a warrant to acquire up to 1,693,745 shares at an exercise price of $200.9266; 307,954 vested immediately and the rest vests in tranches contingent on payments received from the customer, up to a total of $8 billion; it can be exercised until 16 Sep 2033.
  • Capacity. In Q2 2026 the company completed the acquisition of Enercon (East Peoria, Illinois) and acquired an additional facility in Belvidere, Illinois, to expand capacity for large-megawatt generator packaging.

The refund relates to IEEPA tariffs previously paid; the company received about $61.4m in Q2 and recorded a further $27.7m receivable. 2026 guidance: adjusted EBITDA margin of 20–21%, including about 1.5 points from refunds (previous range 18.5–19.5%); net sales growth "mid-to-high teens" maintained.

2. Mix

The segment with the higher margin (Residential) is the one that slipped; the segment that grew (C&I) earns less per dollar of sales. In its Q2 discussion the company says the C&I margin was held back by "an unfavorable sales mix shift and strategic operating expense investments to support future growth".

08Business risks

Status as of 30 Jun 2026 (10-Q), 29 Jul 2026 (release) and 31 Dec 2025 (10-K)

Concentration

The growth rests on one market

Both the 10-K and the 10-Q list "uncertainty regarding the growth of the data center market" among their forward-looking risks. The 10-Q adds, to that list, "contract risk related to terms with certain data center customers, including cancellation rights, delivery requirements, and potential liability exposure tied to our performance obligations or other claimed damages". The 10-K list does not contain it; the 10-Q's Item 1A states there were no material changes to risk factors since the 10-K.

Execution

Capacity has to be built while sales-growth guidance is held

The company is adding capacity for large-megawatt generators (Enercon acquisition completed in Q2; additional Belvidere, Illinois facility) while maintaining 2026 net sales growth guidance of the mid-to-high teens.

Home business

The higher-margin business is not growing

Residential external net sales fell 2.2% in Q2 2026 and 0.6% in the first half. The company says home standby generators have only about 6.75% penetration of the addressable US homes it defines (single-family detached, owner-occupied, home value above $175,000, per the 2023 American Housing Survey) — a statement about the size of the opportunity, not about its pace.
